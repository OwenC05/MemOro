## Literature and design implications

### Retrieval, storage, and dreaming

- **Measure memory at the point of use.** LongMemEval separates extraction, cross-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, updates, and abstention. LongMemEval-V2 reports stronger performance for file-based agentic evidence gathering than its tested retrieval baselines, with a latency cost. These benchmarks cover pieces of task continuity. Compare keyword, hybrid, and agent-driven search with matched budgets, and separately test whether the final task uses correct context. [LongMemEval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10813v2) · [LongMemEval-V2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12493)
- **Treat consolidation as an experiment.** On one Anthropic evaluation, a generated wiki plus search reached 61% as the wiki grew. Search alone scored 53%, and the best wiki-only result was 48%. Another task showed no clear gain from the wiki. Sleep-time Compute studies work done before a query arrives and reports benefits on modified math tasks. Compare later performance and total cost with and without background work. [Anthropic memory experiments, November 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory) · [Sleep-time Compute](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13171)
- **Preserve provenance through edits.** Generative Agents and A-MEM offer precedents for reflection and linked, evolving notes. A 2026 preprint reports that consolidation can keep a statement while losing the limits on its authority. Preserve whether a record came from a user decision, an observed result, a source claim, or an agent inference. Test a retained correction, an equivalent question in a different scope, and a lesson whose underlying evidence changed. [Generative Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442v2) · [A-MEM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12110v11) · [Authority loss during consolidation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01679v2)

### Planning, implementation, and recovery

- **Use proportionate planning.** DORA recommends independently valuable, testable increments. Anthropic's long-running harness work uses structured handoffs and actual end-to-end checks, and its application case study removes some scaffolding as model capability improves. Vendor results establish candidate mechanisms for specific workloads. Compare each mechanism to a capable single agent, and revisit it on model updates. [DORA small batches](https://dora.dev/capabilities/working-in-small-batches/) · [Effective harnesses](https://www.anthropic.com/research/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents) · [Harness design](https://www.anthropic.com/research/harness-design-long-running-apps)
- **Delegate where work separates cleanly.** Controlled scaling experiments find that gains depend on the task and single-agent baseline. Independent research can benefit from parallel exploration, and published engineering accounts describe increased token use and coordination bottlenecks. Specify ownership, interfaces, budgets, and required evidence. Leave the final integration and verification with a clear owner. [Scaling Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08296) · [Controlled study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01268-y) · [Anthropic research system](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multi-agent-research-system) · [Cursor scaling experiments](https://cursor.com/blog/scaling-agents)
- **Keep guidance small and checks executable.** OpenAI's harness engineering case study uses brief entrypoint guidance, repository documentation, executable structural checks, and isolated execution where needed. Skills and lifecycle hooks support progressive disclosure and bounded automation. Permissions and isolation come from the runtime. Verify installed host capabilities before relying on a handoff or a hook. [OpenAI harness engineering](https://openai.com/index/harness-engineering/) · [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) · [Codex hooks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/hooks) · [Orca orchestration](https://www.onorca.dev/docs/cli/orchestration)

### Source-grounded research and useful review

- **Carry claim-level evidence through handoffs.** An EMNLP 2026 study traced citation and faithfulness errors in three deep-research systems. The orchestrator introduced 84.7% of final-report errors in the studied AI-Q configuration. Preserve source version, passage locator, and caveats so the final reviewer can check synthesis against the original material. Use static corpora for controlled tests and a live-web supplement for source freshness and availability. [Citation-error study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24306) · [DR³-Eval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14683) · [DeepResearch Bench II](https://github.com/imlrz/DeepResearch-Bench-II)
- **Verify outcomes with calibrated review.** An observational study of CodeRabbit identifies false positives, redundancy, and scope mismatches among reasons for rejecting reviews. Tie important findings to the affected requirement and corroborating evidence. Verify the resulting code, source claims, or creative artifact using executable checks, source inspection, or human review as appropriate to the domain. [CodeRabbit study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03316v2) · [Demystifying evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
- **Measure the full delivery loop.** METR's early-2025 randomized trial found a 19% slowdown for the studied experienced developers and tasks. Its February 2026 follow-up describes a less reliable estimate under changed selection and concurrent-agent timing. Track human attention, rework and abandoned work alongside elapsed time, cost, and output quality. Inspect evaluator behavior and pin the model, inputs, environment, timeouts, and budget. [METR original trial](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) · [METR follow-up](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/) · [DORA AI tensions](https://dora.dev/insights/balancing-ai-tensions/) · [Agentic Benchmark Checklist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02825) · [Infrastructure noise](https://www.anthropic.com/research/infrastructure-noise)

## Product hypothesis

MemOro can earn a place by carrying current decisions, source evidence, approvals, and verification across projects, sessions, and agent clients. The first useful comparison is a fresh agent resuming real work from a shared project record. Break out the effects of retrieval, planning, delegation, review, and consolidation so results can guide what stays in the workflow. The [benchmark proposal](BENCHMARK.md) translates this hypothesis into software, research, and creative cases.
